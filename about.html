Meet Danny

Lake Washington Garage Door Service is owner-operated by Danny Granfors, who has been repairing and installing garage doors across the Eastside for more than 20 years. When you call, you're talking to the person who does the work — not a call center.

That's the whole idea: quick response, an honest diagnosis, and the best price we can give. We'd rather fix the one part that's failing and earn your next call than oversell you a door you don't need. A lot of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors.

Our trucks are fully stocked, so most repairs happen in a single visit — often the same day. New door? We'll come out, give you a free estimate, and help you choose something that fits your home and budget.
